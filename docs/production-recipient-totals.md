# Recipient totals for decant preparation and labels

In one sales-list cycle, a recipient with incoming gifts has one production portion: their own purchases plus all incoming gift volumes, or gifts alone when there is no own purchase. Different bottle/label instructions do not split this total. The recipient's own request supplies the primary bottle and label instructions; gift-only portions prefer an explicitly selected fancy bottle. Conflicting preparation instructions stay visible beside the total for staff review. Source bottle selections and prices are not changed.

The same grouping serves the live decant roster, archived production roster, label-print roster and generated numeric PDF label entries. Original-bottle owners retain their existing username-only logo label; their displayed production volume includes incoming gifts. Ordinary multiple purchases without incoming gifts retain their existing behavior.

Real numeric Telegram identities take precedence over usernames. Known imported/admin username placeholders can join a recipient's stable identity through unambiguous aliases in that list. Conflicting numeric identities, ambiguous aliases and unknown recipients are not merged by name guessing. Different list cycles, deleted/canceled requests and next-bottle reservations stay separate or excluded as appropriate.

Grouping changes production presentation only. Donor reservations, recipient fields, invoice quantities/prices, bottle charges and payments remain untouched. Public sales-channel posts retain individual reservations. Existing delivered reports or printed PDFs are not silently replaced; explicitly regenerate a report or PDF to use the corrected totals.
